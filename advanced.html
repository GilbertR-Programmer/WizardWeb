We Be Wizards! - Advanced

This advanced page exists for those who want tips on running a WBW game or a deeper look at why certain design choices were made.

Why I made this

We be wizards literally came to me in my dreams. I thought of a system that used 1d4 and could develop naturally as play went on. The base game is extremely narrative but it can be developed with time into a combat heavy game or any other type you like. I like the idea that with WBW players aren't tied to anything specific because of the simple rules and I encourage people tempted to run the game to mess with it as much or as little as they want.

Some tips on running your game

If something in this system doesn't work for you, feel free to change it or look at other systems I'd recommend "Dungeon Game" by Chris Bissette.

Don't get caught up on spells doing the same thing every time. Let your players say what they're using the spells to do and work from there.

Be okay with removing rules you don't like at the end of session, don't stick with a broken rule because you added it.

When adding rules don't be afraid to make ultra specific rules for your game. They don't have to be for every table.

When you are running battles, don't be afraid to implement an initiative system if one is needed for your table, I'd suggest something simple like working clockwise from the game master.

This system is not designed for a specific amount of battles per day, don't feel the need to have a specific amount of combat encounters.

A few rules you could add to your game

Each time you rest for a whole day you undo one hit taken.
When you find yourself in a good position to cast a spell instead roll 2d4 and pick the result you want.
If you as a wizard are trying to do something not obviously easy
roll 1d3 to determine the outcome 1 = Bad 2 = Neutral 3 = Good.
If you are in a magical contest with another wizard, both roll 1d4, higher roll wins the contest.
If you are in dire need you may expend one hit when you cast a spell to modify your roll by one.
If you are in dire need you may expend one hit after you cast a spell to reroll.
You may choose to be a war wizard, you can only cast two spells a day but may take seven hits before you die.
You may choose to be a glass wizard, your spells do two hits when they deal hits but you may only take two hits before you die.
A wizard may only focus on and maintain the effects of one spell at a time.
Two wizards together can choose to cast a combo spell infusing both spells into a single more powerful spell.

My Rules

This is a list of rules me and my friends use in our home game

1 You are a wizard who knows four spells numbered 1-4.
2 To cast a spell roll 1d4, the number you roll determines the spell you cast.
3 If you take four hits you die.
4 At the end of every game session add/change up to two rules, all players must agree on the rules.
5 When you find yourself in a good position to cast a spell instead roll 2d4 and pick the result you want.
6 If you call the spell you roll before rolling and are correct you can change a spell in your list.
